• Antennas can be used for communications, sensing,
  radar, power transfer, imaging, detection, chemical
  differentiation, bio-treatment, etc.

                          Microstrip Antenna
A microstrip antenna (or patch antenna) is a low-profile antenna built on a
printed circuit board (PCB) in a sandwich-like structure.

                                         Radiating Patch (Top Layer): A thin metallic layer
                                         made of conductive material like copper or gold

                                         Dielectric Substrate (Middle Layer): A non-
                                         conductive material (such as FR4) with a specific
                                         dielectric constant and thickness

                                         Ground Plane (Bottom Layer): A solid metallic layer
                                         covering the entire bottom surface of the dielectric
                                         substrate

                                         Feed Line: The connection technique used to transfer
                                         radio frequency (RF) energy into the radiating patch


               First proposed in 1953, became popular in 1970s
