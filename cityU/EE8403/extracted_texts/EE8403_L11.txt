Concept of stability
 Stability considers:
 1. Can you fix the system at a steady operating point?
 2. Consider a “perturbation” added at the operating point, will the system be fixed (at the
 same point or a different point) again?

         Excitation control of a synchronous machine (generator or condenser)

• A synchronous machine presents an internal voltage source and thus provide direct
  support to system voltage
• The amplitude of the internal voltage is controlled by the excitor: field current (IF) →
  flux (φ) → voltage (E)
• The field current IF has constraint: after reaching the maximum IF, the voltage control
  capability is limited
• A synchronous condenser is a synchronous machine that does not generate power:
