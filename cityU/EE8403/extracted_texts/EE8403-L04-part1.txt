N-gram Language Models
• What?
     
A system that 
assigns a probability to a piece of text
 (a sentence, a phrase).
     
It answers: "How likely is this sequence of words to appear in a given language?”
• Example:
     
Machine Translation: 
"The cat sat on the mat"
 is more probable than 
"Mat the on sat cat the"
.
     Speech Recognition: 
"Recognize speech"
 sounds like 
"Wreck a nice beach"
. An LM helps choose the correct one.

Bigram (2-gram):
 "the cat", "cat sat"
Trigram (3-gram):
 "the cat sat"
