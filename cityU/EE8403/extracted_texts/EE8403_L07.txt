What deployment tests tell us
 • Sensitivity analysis
    – Speaker volume (works in 20% to 100% phone volume)
    – ELF sequence length (best when ELF length >= 0.6s)
    – Nearby moving people (up to 4 people in 60𝑚2 living room)
    – Map aging (no salient aging issue within one month)
    – Audible noise (robust to audible noise)
    – Space layout changes (tolerate up to 4 objects movements in 60𝑚2 living room)
 • System overhead
    – Localization response time on smartphone: ~1.1s
    – Processor utilization: ~20%
    – Network bandwidth usage: 90kbps
    – Battery usage: 270mAh (Google map is 280mAh)
