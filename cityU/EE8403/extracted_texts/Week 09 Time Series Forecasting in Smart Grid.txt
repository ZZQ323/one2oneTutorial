Time series definition




 Observations are ordered chronologically, usually at a fixed sampling interval.
 Each interval contains at most one observation, and the recorded data may contain missing
 values.

The composition of time series


                          Trend: A long-term increase or decrease.

                          Seasonality: Variations that repeat at regular intervals.

                          Cyclical variation: Fluctuations whose duration is not
                          necessarily fixed.

                          Random variation: Unexplained disturbances after the
                          systematic components have been removed.
