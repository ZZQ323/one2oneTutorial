Bidding must balance market opportunity and deliverability

                                                                                                      Price spikes

   ① Market uncertainty
   •   Energy and FCAS prices are highly volatile, with contingency FCAS
       subject to sudden price spikes.
   •   Forecast errors introduce substantial uncertainty into bidding returns.
   •   External events, such as reserve scarcity, network contingencies,
       and extreme weather, can further alter market conditions and
       increase price-spike risks.
   ② Limited BESS flexibility
   •   BESS operation is constrained by finite energy capacity and State of
       Charge (SoC).
   •   Charging and discharging are subject to power limits and
       degradation costs.
   •   Energy and multiple FCAS services compete for the same limited
       battery capacity, requiring careful capacity allocation.

          Therefore, BESS bidding is not only a profit-maximization problem, but a sequential risk-
          sensitive decision-making problem under market uncertainty.

Domain-specific agent design

 Each agent specializes in a specific domain to achieve deep expertise:

 Generation-Side Agents
 • Renewable Forecasting Agent: Solar/wind power prediction with uncertainty quantification
 • Thermal Unit Agent: Conventional generator optimization and commitment decisions
 • Energy Storage Agent: Battery and pumped-storage optimization strategies
 Grid-Side Agents
 • State Estimation Agent: Real-time system monitoring and bad data detection
 • Power Flow Agent: Optimal power flow calculation and congestion management
 • Security Analysis Agent: N-1 contingency analysis and preventive control
 Market-Side Agents
 • Price Forecasting Agent: Electricity price prediction using market intelligence
 • Bidding Strategy Agent: Ancillary service bidding optimization
 • Contract Management Agent: Bilateral contract optimization and risk management

more informative of sudden price spikes. By dynamically shifting the risk preference, the LLM-adjusted
